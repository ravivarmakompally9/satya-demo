AI-DRIVEN VERIFICATION FOR HIGH-RISK BUSINESS REQUESTS

Verify who authorized what, before money moves.

SATYA holds high-risk payments and vendor bank changes inside the finance system, links each request to security and finance signals, and releases money only after the right person is verified through a trusted route.

Callback script · written by SATYA Analyst

  2. "Did your company ask us to change bank details on 16 October?"
  3. "What is the amount on invoice INV-7731?" (expected ₹8,00,000)

Why approval is bound to the payment

An approval is a short-lived token tied to this payment's amount, account and version. FinLedger checks it again at the moment of release. If anything changes, the approval is cancelled and verification starts again.

PAY-24817 (₹40 lakh to Apex Traders) does not appear here. Tier 4 payments can't be approved; they go to the security team.

Tip: enter any six digits to approve, then try editing the amount.
